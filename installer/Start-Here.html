OPENLOCKER / 0.1.2

A little privacy,
at your fingertips.

Lock Mac apps and Chrome websites with your Mac's Touch ID.

Set up your privacy lock

  1. Open Install OpenLocker.app on this disk.
  2. Click Install OpenLocker. The Mac app is installed in your Applications folder; the Touch ID helper and extension are copied to your user Library. No Terminal, Xcode, or administrator password.
  3. For Mac apps, click Open OpenLocker and use its menu-bar icon. Allow Accessibility when asked, then click a window to protect its app.
  4. For Chrome websites, click Copy folder path & open Chrome. Turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, then press ⇧⌘G and paste the copied path.

Pin OpenLocker. Visit a website. Click Lock this site.

If macOS blocks the app

This release uses a persistent self-signed certificate and is not notarized by Apple. Upgrading from 0.1.0 requires a one-time Accessibility renewal; OpenLocker guides you through it. macOS may require you to approve the app in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Follow Apple's guidance for opening an app from an unidentified developer only if you trust this download. OpenLocker never changes Gatekeeper settings.

Requires macOS 13+, Google Chrome 120+, and enrolled Touch ID. Universal app: Apple Silicon + Intel. After setup, you can eject this disk. Keep the installed extension folder in your Library.
