Connecting to the OpenLocker Mac helper.

  1. Download the OpenLocker Mac installer.
  2. Open the installer and click Install OpenLocker.
  3. Come back here and click Check again. Then pin OpenLocker in Chrome's Extensions menu.

Requires macOS 13+ and enrolled Touch ID. No developer tools needed. The helper runs locally and never sees your fingerprint.

    Nothing locked yet.
    Visit a site and click OpenLocker → Lock this site.

    Each lock includes subdomains. Unlocking applies to one tab and relocks when you switch tabs or Chrome windows, leave the domain, restart Chrome, or lock your Mac. Locking replaces open pages, so save any drafts first. Removing a site requires Touch ID.

    OpenLocker adds privacy inside this Chrome profile. It cannot prevent someone from disabling the extension or using another browser. Incognito and Chrome's own pages are not covered. Privacy & scope ↗
